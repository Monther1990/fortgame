# Assets notice

- **Characters (boy, girl, Cyber):** original 3D models built from the Cyber Aware owner's own character sheets; shared by
  every game in the series.
- **3D kit (`public/assets/kit.glb`):** original Omani doors, props and furniture made for the series (palms used here).
- **Rustaq Fort (`public/assets/fort.glb`):** modelled in Blender by `pipeline/build_fort.py` from the owner's reference
  photo of the National Museum model of قلعة الرستاق (`references/rustaq-fort-model.png`); a stylised game model of a
  public heritage site.
- **Towers, threats, pads and the chest (`public/assets/units.glb`):** original designs, `pipeline/build_units.py`.
- **Flag of the Sultanate of Oman:** drawn in code (`src/fort/flag.ts`), shown with respect on the fort.
- **Village houses and watch towers (`public/assets/race-kit.glb`):** from the series' Cyber Rally kit (original).
- **Font:** Readex Pro, SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`public/fonts/OFL.txt`).
- **Music, ambience, effects and caller voice:** generated in the owner's ElevenLabs workspace; commercial use follows the
  owner's ElevenLabs plan. List any new sound added for this game here.
- **Code dependency:** Babylon.js (Apache-2.0).
- **Content:** all names, addresses, codes and links in the missions are fictional (`*.internal` domains, simulated codes).
